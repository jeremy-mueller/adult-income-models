## Overview / Problem Statement
This project evaluates two computer models designed to predict whether a person earns over $50,000 a year using U.S. Census data. We evaluated these models under a wealth management marketing scenario. While automated tools help target marketing offers, using an unverified model can waste marketing funds or leave out eligible people. We focused our audit on two main goals: making sure the models rely on real economic patterns instead of technical shortcuts, and checking if they identify prospective clients fairly across gender and racial groups.
